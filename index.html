Overview

One dashboard, many questions

The project examines how petrol, diesel and LPG prices relate to taxation structures, subsidy levels and national income, and what that means for economic and environmental outcomes. Geographic maps, trend lines with forecasts, comparative bars and heat maps work together in a single Tableau dashboard.

Prices

Petrol, diesel and LPG per litre in USD, by country, region and month.

Taxes

Average tax percentage of the pump price, compared across regions and income groups.

Subsidies

Subsidy levels from low to very high, and how they pull prices down.

Income & region

Low, middle and high income nations across seven regions side by side.

Who it is for

Three people, three lenses

Energy Policy Consultant

Advises government agencies on fuel taxation and subsidy policy, and needs defensible numbers to balance growth with environmental responsibility.

  • Compare regional tax trends and spot policy disparities (Tax Map, Tax vs Subsidy)
  • See how subsidy level changes fuel prices (Subsidy vs Price)
  • Present clear evidence to government bodies and stakeholders
Global Supply Chain Manager

Runs multi-continent logistics and must manage exposure to fuel price swings to keep transport costs down without hurting service.

  • Find regions with favourable pricing (Top 10 / Bottom 10 countries)
  • Judge long-term direction with the diesel forecast
  • Assess risk with the volatility heat map
Energy Economics Researcher

Studies how income, taxes and subsidies shape fuel prices, aiming to publish findings and guide development organisations.

  • Test whether high-income nations tax differently (Tax by Income)
  • Examine subsidy mix by income group (Subsidy by Income)
  • Compare diesel, LPG and petrol prices by income (Price by Income)

Selected findings

What the dashboard shows

Most expensive diesel (avg USD per litre)

Average tax % by income level

High-income countries tax fuel noticeably more heavily than low- and middle-income ones.

Subsidies and prices move in opposite directions

In the Subsidy vs Price view, average fuel prices fall steeply as subsidy levels rise: low-subsidy groups sit around 3 USD per litre in several regions, while very-high-subsidy groups drop to a fraction of a dollar.

Method

Built with

Data from a global fuel prices dataset (27,468 complete records, 84 countries, 2020–2026), analysed and visualised in Tableau Public. The dashboard combines geographic maps, time-series forecasting, ranked bars, stacked proportions and heat maps, with filters linking the views so each persona can follow their own question.
